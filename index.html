About the Firm

Precision is not
an upgrade. It's the job.

Omalbushra Construction Company was built on a simple standard: measure twice, build once, and never hand over a site you wouldn't move your own family into.

We take on residential builds, commercial fit-outs, renovations and ground-up developments — managing everything from permits and structural engineering to the final coat of paint. Every project is run by a dedicated site lead who answers your calls, not a call centre.

What sets us apart isn't the size of the crew — it's the size of the tolerance we work within. Level floors. Square corners. Foundations checked against drawings, not guesswork.

How a Build Runs

Four stages. No shortcuts.

    Site Survey & Quote

    We walk the site, review drawings or help draft them, and return a fixed, itemized quote — not a ballpark.

  • 02

    Permits & Planning

    Approvals, scheduling and material orders locked in before a single tool touches the ground.

  • 03

    Build & Report

    Construction proceeds in stages with weekly progress reports and photos sent directly to you.

  • 04

    Inspection & Handover

    Final walkthrough, snag list closed out, and a 10-year structural warranty on handover.
